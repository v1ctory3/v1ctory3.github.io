Seungchan Woo

Ph.D. Student · Computer and Information Security · Sejong University

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer and Information Security at Sejong University, advised by Prof. Jong-Hyouk Lee. I have been a researcher at the Protocol Engineering Lab (PEL) since July 2021.

My research focuses on security and trust in next-generation networks, particularly 5G-Advanced/6G, Open RAN, and UAV-mounted base stations (UxNBs). I investigate distributed ledger-based trust models for UxNB services, network attack scenarios, and security-by-design. My broader interests include decentralized identity, privacy protection, and copyright protection.

Alongside my research, I contribute to domestic and international standardization for drone and Counter-UAS systems. My work addresses interoperability, functional requirements, data exchange interfaces, and conformance testing, connecting security research with practical system requirements.

Research interests

  • Wireless and network security: 5G-Advanced/6G, Open RAN, UxNBs, and security-by-design.
  • Drone and Counter-UAS systems: Drone cybersecurity, interoperability, data exchange interfaces, and conformance testing.
  • Decentralized trust and privacy: Blockchain/DLT, decentralized identifiers, and copyright protection.
  • Cybersecurity standardization: Security requirements and domestic and international standards for emerging ICT systems.

International research experience

  • Ruhr University Bochum, Germany — Visiting Researcher, December 2025–May 2026. Worked with Prof. Tarik Taleb on distributed ledger-based trust models and security for UxNB services in 6G Open RAN environments.
  • University of Oulu, Finland — Visiting Researcher, September 2023. Conducted attack experiments and data collection using the Open Source 5GTN testbed as part of Korea–Finland joint research on security datasets.

Standardization activities

  • TTA/TC 4/PG 428 — Drone System, committee member, February 2025–present. Contributions to domestic anti-drone system standards, including interfaces and data specifications.
  • ISO/TC 20/SC 16/WG 8 — Counter UAS, working group member, June 2026–present. Contributions to ISO 25461 on functional requirements for detection, tracking, and identification, and a proposed standard on the corresponding data exchange interfaces.
  • ISO/TC 20/SC 16/WG 10 — Cybersecurity, working group member, July 2026–present. Participation in international standardization activities on cybersecurity for uncrewed aircraft systems.
