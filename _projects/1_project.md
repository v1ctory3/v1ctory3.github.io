**Status: Ongoing**

UxNBs bring radio access capabilities to unmanned aerial vehicles, supporting connectivity where terrestrial infrastructure is unavailable or disrupted. Their mobility, wireless backhaul, and physical exposure introduce challenges for both network performance and security.

My research investigates how these systems can provide reliable connectivity while remaining resilient to attacks. I combine experimental work on mobile networks with the study of positioning and service continuity in tactical and emergency scenarios.

## Current research

- **Measurement-driven positioning:** investigating how network measurement reports can guide UxNB placement and improve radio coverage.
- **Security evaluation:** studying threats to UAV-based base stations and their interaction with the mobile core network.
- **Experimental testbeds:** developing and using 5G-Advanced environments to examine network behavior and attack scenarios.
